03 Services

Carpentry across the build.

From the structural frame to decking, pergolas, cladding and the external carpentry that closes up a building, for projects across Sydney and throughout NSW. Scope is agreed with each client before work begins.

S/01

Timber framing

Structural timber framing for residential projects: wall frames, roof framing and associated carpentry, set out to the documented design and coordinated with the builder's program.

Enquire about this service: Timber framing
S/02

New builds

Carpentry for new residential construction, from frame and roof structure through to the external carpentry that closes up the build.

Enquire about this service: New builds
S/03

Extensions

Framing and carpentry for additions to existing buildings, tying new structure into the old with attention to levels, alignment and weatherproof junctions.

Enquire about this service: Extensions
S/04

Granny flats

Carpentry for secondary dwellings: compact layouts where every wall, opening and roof line has to make good use of the space available.

Enquire about this service: Granny flats
S/05

Eaves

Eave framing, lining and associated external carpentry, finished square and true so that fascia, gutter and roofing trades can follow on cleanly.

Enquire about this service: Eaves
S/06

Renovations

Carpentry for alterations and improvements to existing buildings, carried out with care around existing structure, finishes and occupied spaces.

Enquire about this service: Renovations
S/07

Decking

Timber decking and associated carpentry designed to create practical, well-finished outdoor living spaces. Scope may include decking installation, substructure and associated carpentry, subject to project requirements.

Enquire about this service: Decking
S/08

Pergolas

Pergola construction and associated carpentry to create functional outdoor areas, with design, dimensions, materials and installation requirements confirmed for each project.

Enquire about this service: Pergolas
S/09

Cladding

External cladding installation and associated carpentry, coordinated with the building design, specified materials and project requirements.

Enquire about this service: Cladding
S/10

Cut on site

Members measured, cut and fitted on site to suit the structure as it stands, keeping fit-up accurate and allowing adjustments where site conditions differ from the plan.

Enquire about this service: Cut on site

06 About

Practical craftsmanship. Built around the project.

Makkah Carpentry & Construction Pty Ltd is a Sydney-based carpentry and construction business offering services across timber framing, new builds, extensions, granny flats, eaves, renovations, decking, pergolas and cladding. We undertake projects across Sydney and throughout New South Wales, and are willing to travel for suitable projects in regional and rural NSW.

The work is practical and site-based: members are cut and fitted on site, scope is agreed before work begins, and progress is coordinated with the builder and the trades that follow. The aim on every job is a structure that is square, true and ready for the next stage of the build.

We work with builders, developers and private clients, and welcome enquiries from councils, government agencies, commercial project teams and property managers throughout NSW.

  • Clear communication

    Scope, timing and any changes discussed openly and early.

  • Attention to detail

    Set-out and fit-up checked as the work goes in.

  • Coordination

    Working around the builder's program and the trades on site.

  • Quality focus

    Work finished to the documented design and agreed standard.
