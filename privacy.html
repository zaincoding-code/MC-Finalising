Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Makkah Carpentry & Construction Pty Ltd ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we receive through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

We only receive the details you choose to send us, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, project type, project area and location, any site access or travel notes, and a description of your project. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How enquiries reach us

The enquiry form does not store or send your details itself. When you press the button, it opens your own email app with your enquiry written out and addressed to Makkahcarpentry@outlook.com. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app. Your email is then handled by your email provider and ours, like any other email.

You can also contact us directly by phone on 0414 955 844 or by email.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare quotes or company information you request.

We do not sell your information or add you to marketing lists.

Storage and security

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond to you and keep reasonable business records, and we take reasonable steps to protect them.

Access and correction

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing Makkahcarpentry@outlook.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Makkahcarpentry@outlook.com or by calling 0414 955 844.
